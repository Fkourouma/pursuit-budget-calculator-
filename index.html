Income Information

Bond Payment Rules:

  • Payments start at $85,000+ annual salary
  • Payment = 15% of monthly gross income
  • Payments end after: 36 monthly payments, 5 years, or $55,000 total
ℹ️ Why 15%? The 15% payment is designed to be manageable while helping you fulfill your commitment to Pursuit.
